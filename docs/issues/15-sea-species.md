# 15 Sea species

Type: HITL. Phase 2. Blocked by: 05, 09, and a design session. Read `docs/fauna.md`.

## What to build

Animals in the water on open-sea and coastal sites. The globe already rolls `sea` niche species that use `fins` and other locomotions. On the ground they swim under a translucent sea and surface now and then.

## Needs a decision first

- Whether sea species are their own class in `species.js` or the existing `sea` niche on the `air` and `sub` classes.
- How the camera treats the water: does it stay above the surface, or can it go under.
- Whether the sea plane needs a depth cue for animals under it.

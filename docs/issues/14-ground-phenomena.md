# 14 Ground-scale phenomena

Type: HITL. Phase 2. Blocked by: 04, and a design session. Read `docs/probe.md` decision 6.

## What to build

The one phenomenon of the world at metre scale when the site is pulled to it: a volcano cone with a crater, smoke, and embers; a geyser vent that erupts; a fissure with glow; an aurora overhead; a storm cell with bolts. Once this exists, the pull in issue 02 includes the phenomenon site among its targets.

## Needs a decision first

- Which of the five kinds come first. The volcano and the geyser reuse the most from `phenomena.js`.
- How the patch terrain gets the cone or the vent. Likely a shape term added to the heights in the worker, as the globe does with `makeActivity`.
- Whether the aurora and the storm play everywhere on the world or only near the pulled site.

# Chapters open in strict order

The chapters of a world open in their fixed order. A chapter opens when the chapter before it ends, and a closed chapter cannot end. A tap on the source of a closed search marks the source, but no card opens and no find is recorded.

This supersedes decision 12 of p2-00 and the find by chance of p2-42. There, a find of the ruin before the wreck tuned the world, and the receiver then went back to the wreck. We chose strict order for two reasons. The story then reads in one order, and the module of the chapters needs no rule for a find out of order.

A record from an older build can hold a find of the ruin before the find of the wreck. That record keeps its find, and the chapter of the ruin shows as done when it opens.

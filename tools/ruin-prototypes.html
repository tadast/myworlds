The spine

These six steps hold for every choice below. Steps 3 to 5 reuse the search of issue 34 as it is: the reach, the error of 2 to 10 degrees, four fixes, and the wedges painted on the terrain.

  1. The log ends on the signal

    The last entry states a frequency, and the crew says what they will do about it. The number prints in no other entry.

  2. The reader tunes the receiver

    A field takes the number. The right number locks the receiver on the second source. The store keeps the tune.

  3. Probe

    A landing hears the new carrier and states a bearing and an error. The first fix usually comes at the wreck itself.

  4. Wedge

    The globe paints the fixes of chapter 2 in a second colour. The mini wreck stays on its cell.

  5. Cross

    Two or three landings close the wedges on one cell. Near it, the range shows. On it, the needle points at the structure.

  6. The structure

    A tap on it opens its card, and the find leaves a second mini model on the globe.
